# Meal plan — Wed 01 Jul (restart day)

Small cut · high protein · eating at home. Lift: upper body + posterior.

## Breakfast — sorted ✅

Sourdough + cottage cheese, and homemade coffee with 100ml of the 3% milk. Simple, works, tides you over the morning.

## Lunch — sorted ✅

Chicken salad — assemble chicken at lunch, grab some veggies.

## Afternoon snack — sorted ✅

Melon + cottage cheese. Good protein, hits the fresh/sweet craving on a cut.

## Dinner — chicken night ✅

**Roast chicken thighs + breast** with roast potatoes / sweet potato + veg.
- Season thighs + breast (paprika, garlic, salt, pepper, olive oil)
- Thighs ~25–30 min, breast pulled earlier so it stays juicy
- Roast potatoes / sweet potato + a tray of veg alongside (from the shop below)
- **Cook extra** → leftover chicken = meal-prep protein for the next days

## Overnight oats — assemble tonight (for Thu breakfast)

In a jar:
- 50g rolled oats
- 150–200ml milk (or half milk / half Greek yogurt)
- 1 scoop protein powder *or* extra yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia
- berries / half a banana

Stir, lid on, fridge overnight. Done for Thursday's office morning.

## 🛒 Veggie shop

**Salad / fresh:** cucumber, tomatoes, salad leaves, red onion, peppers
**Roasting:** potatoes / sweet potato, broccoli, courgette, carrots, red onion
**Greens / extras:** spinach, garlic, lemons
**Fruit:** melon (for the snack), berries (oats + breakfast)
