## Overnight oats — assemble tonight (for Thu breakfast)

In a jar:
- 50g rolled oats
- 150–200ml milk (or half milk / half Greek yogurt)
- 1 scoop protein powder *or* extra yogurt
- 1 tbsp chia
- berries / half a banana

Stir, lid on, fridge overnight. Done for Thursday's office morning.
